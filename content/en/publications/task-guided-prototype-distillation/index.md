## Research summary

This manuscript studies how to compress large-scale lithium-ion battery aging data into a small number of learnable prototypes while preserving regression relationships, cross-sample distributions, and degradation-sequence structure.

The reported approach assigns SOH semantics with anchors and Huber regression loss, aligns feature distributions with Maximum Mean Discrepancy (MMD), and uses similarity constraints plus 2D total variation regularization. Voltage, current, and time sequences are transformed into Gramian Angular Field (GAF) images and evaluated with a fixed ResNet-2D network. The submitted-manuscript HNEL setup reports MAE 0.0192 with three synthetic prototypes.
