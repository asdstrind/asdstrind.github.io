## Research contribution

The paper develops a unified modeling approach for heterogeneous lithium-ion battery data, combining multimodal representation learning with lightweight parameter-efficient fine-tuning. It evaluates the model across a wide range of degradation datasets and connects joint SOH/RUL prediction with condition monitoring and predictive maintenance.
