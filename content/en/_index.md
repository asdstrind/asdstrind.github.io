        **Zihan Gong**

        **Undergraduate Researcher at Xi’an Jiaotong University**

        Intelligent Manufacturing Engineering · Qian Xuesen Honor Class, Automation Track

        I am an undergraduate in Intelligent Manufacturing Engineering in the Qian Xuesen Honor Class at Xi’an Jiaotong University. I study data-efficient learning and cross-domain generalization for battery health management. My work covers lithium-ion battery State-of-Health (SOH) estimation, cross-domain representation learning, dataset and prototype distillation, and multimodal foundation models for joint SOH and Remaining Useful Life (RUL) prediction. I build Python/PyTorch data pipelines, align feature distributions with Maximum Mean Discrepancy (MMD), and develop lightweight CNN–Transformer–KAN models and learnable prototypes, evaluating parameter count, computational cost, and inference latency.
