## Description

Contributed to a kinematic model for missile-UAV-smoke projectile systems and used a line-segment-to-sphere intersection criterion to determine line-of-sight occlusion.

## Methods

- Compared exhaustive search, PSO, GA, and a coupled PSO-GA optimizer for a high-dimensional non-convex optimization problem.
- Implemented the algorithms in C++ with `iostream`, `cmath`, and `algorithm`.
- Generated convergence curves and result charts and participated in manuscript writing.
