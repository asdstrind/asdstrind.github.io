## Description

Participated in the implementation and validation of a single-template retrieval-based wheel hub recognition scheme and reviewed the Python preprocessing, descriptor extraction, recognition, and evaluation modules.

## Methods

- Worked with the WheelPrint and RSM-HOG feature-fusion pipeline and its rejection mechanism.
- Authored technical documentation covering design, testing, diagnosis, and improvement.
- Participated in validating experimental data and summarizing misclassification cases caused by strong light reflections.

## Outcome

The fusion scheme achieved 97.62% Top-1 accuracy on the development set. With rejection enabled, accepted samples achieved 100% accuracy at 79.76% coverage, with a median CPU latency of 226 ms per image.
