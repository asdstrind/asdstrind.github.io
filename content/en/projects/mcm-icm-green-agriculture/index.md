## Description

Contributed to a multi-species reaction-diffusion model incorporating Lotka-Volterra-type predation, competition, and cooperation.

- Performed numerical simulations, parameter sweeps, and visualization in MATLAB R2023a.
- Co-authored the competition manuscript and typeset it in LaTeX.
